The problem

Finding a tender is easy. Checking eligibility is the hard part.

GeM lists hundreds of open bids per product category. The turnover threshold, years of experience, OEM authorisation and EMD sit inside each bid PDF, and the MSE and startup relaxations come with conditions. A small supplier has to check all of it against their own records before deciding to bid.

Four possible verdicts

When the evidence isn't clean, BidReady says so.

A well-formed answer from the AI is not proof the bid says it. A quote not found on its page stays visible and unresolved instead of being guessed. A criterion the bid does not set is marked Not required.

Eligible

Every requirement the bid sets is met, each backed by a quote from the bid.

Eligible with relaxation

The bid grants an MSE or startup relaxation, your status and role qualify, and the proof is on file.

Not eligible

A requirement fails and no relaxation in the bid covers it. Traders get no MSE EMD exemption.

Needs manual review

A quote could not be verified, or a condition needs a person to read the PDF.

Trust standard

The model extracts. Our code decides.

  • Every quote is checked against the text of its page in the bid PDF before any verdict.
  • Fixed rules decide each criterion, including the bid's own clause that excludes traders from the MSE EMD exemption.
  • Supplier-declared facts and document-verified evidence stay distinct.
  • Web signals from TinyFish come with their snippet and link for a person to check; they never change a verdict.
  • Past-performance share, OEM turnover, technical specs and delivery are listed as conditions for a person to check.
